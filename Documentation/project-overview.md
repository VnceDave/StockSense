# StockSense: Inventory and Supplier Management 

## 1. System Explanation

StockSense is a web-based Inventory and Supplier Management System designed for small food businesses such as cafés, bakeries, and carinderias. Its main purpose is to help business owners and staff manage their ingredients, monitor stock levels, track expiration dates, and organize supplier information in one system.

Instead of manually recording inventory in notebooks or spreadsheets, users can use StockSense to record and update their ingredients more easily. Each ingredient will have a unique identification code, such as **ING-001 for Flour** and **ING-002 for Sugar**, making it easier to identify and manage inventory records.

The system includes a dashboard that displays important information, such as the total number of ingredients, low-stock items, ingredients that are about to expire, and registered suppliers. It also has a stock-in and stock-out feature for recording ingredients received from suppliers and ingredients used or removed from inventory.

Another feature is batch tracking, which allows users to record batch numbers and expiration dates. This helps staff identify ingredients that need to be used soon and reduces the risk of using expired ingredients. The system also provides low-stock alerts to notify users when certain ingredients need to be restocked.

StockSense also includes supplier management, purchase order recording, and report generation. Users can maintain supplier information, record orders for ingredients, and generate inventory reports that can be printed or exported to Excel.

Overall, StockSense aims to make inventory management more organized, accurate, and efficient for small food businesses. It helps users monitor their available ingredients, reduce inventory recording errors, avoid unnecessary waste, and make better decisions when purchasing supplies.

## 2. Main Objectives

1. To develop a web-based system for managing ingredients and inventory records.
2. To monitor stock levels and provide alerts for low-stock ingredients.
3. To track ingredient batches and expiration dates.
4. To organize supplier information and purchase order records.
5. To generate inventory reports for easier monitoring and decision-making.

## 3. Target Users

- **Business Owner or Administrator:** Manages inventory records, suppliers, purchase orders, and reports.
- **Staff or Inventory Personnel:** Records stock-in and stock-out transactions, checks available ingredients, and monitors expiration dates.

## 4. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional and user-friendly inventory management system that helps small food businesses organize their ingredients and supplier records. Through StockSense, users can monitor their inventory more conveniently and identify which ingredients need restocking or require attention because of their expiration dates.
