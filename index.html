FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?

Here are some things you might want to know about StockSense.

What is StockSense?

StockSense is an inventory and supplier management system designed for small food businesses.

Who can use StockSense?

StockSense is designed for cafés, bakeries, carinderias, and other small food businesses.

Can I monitor my ingredient stocks?

Yes. You can manage inventory records and monitor available stock quantities.

Does StockSense provide stock alerts?

Yes. The system can notify users about low-stock items and ingredients nearing expiration.
